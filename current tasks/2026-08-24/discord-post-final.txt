Hi all — first, thanks: this only exists because of NIR/NIRTorch, snnTorch, Brian2, PyNN, Sinabs, Rockpool, Lava and NeuroBench.

I built it in response to the Dutch neuromorphic alliance (NC-NL), whose white paper keeps naming the same bottleneck: hardware setup, drivers and simulators eat weeks, and there's little usable tooling on top. Six months later, a proof of concept:

🎥 3-min demo: [video URL]

Design an SNN visually or in controlled English, train it once in snnTorch, run it through NIR on several platforms — including a real Akida card. Three runtimes plus Akida execute today; the rest is in progress.

Two questions:

**1.** Has anyone systematically measured what survives across NIR conversion paths? In my pipeline I found silent semantic loss — e.g. shape survived while trained weights did not, so simulators ran a zero-weight network without failing. I'm considering this as my MSc thesis.

**2.** I'm a software engineer (8 yrs) coming into neuromorphic from outside, building this alongside a full-time job. What evidence would make a project like this useful to you: fidelity tests, reproducible benchmarks, or broader hardware coverage?

Solo project, soon open source. I used AI agents heavily for implementation; I set the architecture and tests and own the hardware debugging and verification.
