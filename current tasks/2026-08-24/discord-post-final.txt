Hi all — first, thanks: this only exists because of work from this community. NIR/NIRTorch, snnTorch, Brian2, PyNN, Sinabs, Rockpool, Lava, NeuroBench.

I built it around the bottlenecks **Neuromorphic Computing NL** (NC-NL, the Dutch national alliance) identified in their white paper, roadmap and action plan: hardware is hard to set up, getting drivers, simulators and remote servers working takes weeks, and there's almost no standardisation or usable software on top. I ran into exactly that myself. Six months later, a proof of concept:

🎥 3-min demo: [video URL]

Design an SNN visually or in controlled English, train it once in snnTorch, run it through NIR on several platforms — including a real Akida card. PoC status: three runtimes plus Akida actually execute, the rest is in progress.

Two things I'd value:

**1.** Feedback on the NIR round-trip. I hit a lot of *silent* failures — e.g. a spec that carried shape but not trained weights, so the simulators happily ran a network of zeros. Which of these are known, and has anyone systematically measured what survives a NIR round trip? I'm considering it as my MSc thesis.

**2.** I'm a software engineer (8 yrs) coming into neuromorphic from outside, building this alongside a full-time job. For someone making that move — what would you actually want to see in a project like this?

Solo project, will be open source, built fast with heavy AI-agent assistance against an architecture and tests I set. Architecture, hardware debugging and verification are mine.
