# Subject: Application for Prophesee Developer Program - Integrating NMTK with Metavision

Neuromorphic computing is the future of efficient AI, but its widespread adoption is bottlenecked by a fragmented, multidisciplinary ecosystem. Neuroscientists understand the biology, hardware engineers build the chips, and software developers write the pipelines—but they all use different, disjointed tools.

To solve this, I am developing the **NeuroMorphicToolkit (NMTK)**. NMTK is a unified, cross-platform desktop application designed to bridge the gap between these disciplines. Orchestrated via a local micro-service architecture (a Flutter frontend managing Docker/Python backends), NMTK provides a cohesive framework for neuromorphic development. It seamlessly integrates modules for plain-English to SNN translation (NeuroCNL), sensory spike encoding (Neurosense), hardware interfacing (Neurochip), and performance benchmarking (Neurobench). My objective is to completely lower the barrier to entry, allowing researchers and developers to deploy models to edge devices without juggling fragmented repositories and dependency hell.

I am an independent software developer and founder based in the Netherlands, bringing 8 years of professional mobile software development experience to the table. I hold a Master's degree in Psychology, which gives me a strong, intuitive understanding of cognitive structures, biological behaviors, and brain plasticity. I am currently expanding this by pursuing a second Master's degree in Applied A.I. at JKU in Linz, Austria. Running my own one-person company has taught me how to be self-reliant and take a product entirely from a conceptual vision to a fully deployed application. My background is uniquely suited for the neuromorphic engineering space: I combine the psychological understanding of biological invariants with the rigorous, production-ready engineering skills needed to build robust, user-friendly software infrastructure.

Prophesee's event-based sensors are revolutionizing machine vision by mimicking the human retina. My toolkit features a dedicated module called **Neurosense** specifically engineered for sensory processing and encoding traditional and event-based data into spike trains for SNNs. I want to leverage your development program to deeply integrate the Metavision SDK into NMTK. My 8 years of mobile development experience and product-focused approach running my own company allows me to build native, rapid-prototyping interfaces that will help developers test and integrate Prophesee sensors into their hardware seamlessly.
