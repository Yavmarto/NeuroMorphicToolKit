## BrainChip — Akida

**Subject: Developer Program Application — Building Akida Integration into NMTK**

Neuromorphic computing is the future of efficient AI, but its widespread adoption is bottlenecked by a fragmented, multidisciplinary ecosystem. Neuroscientists understand the biology, hardware engineers build the chips, and software developers write the pipelines — but they all use different, disjointed tools.

To solve this, I am building the **NeuroMorphicToolkit (NMTK)**: a unified, cross-platform desktop application that bridges these disciplines through a local micro-service architecture — a Flutter frontend orchestrating Docker/Python backends. NMTK is under active development, with modules for plain-English to SNN translation (NeuroCNL), sensory spike encoding (Neurosense), hardware interfacing (Neurochip), and performance benchmarking (Neurobench). The goal is to let researchers and developers deploy models to edge devices without juggling fragmented repositories and dependency hell.

I am an independent software developer and founder based in the Netherlands, with 8 years of professional mobile software development experience. I hold a Master's degree in Psychology and am currently completing a second Master's in Applied A.I. at JKU Linz — alongside a full-time job. NMTK is built in the margins: evenings, weekends, and whatever time I can carve out. I am doing this deliberately, because neuromorphic computing is the field I am moving into full-time. This project is my starting point — the thing I am building to prove to myself and to this industry that I belong here. My background bridges the psychological understanding of biological invariants with the production-grade engineering discipline needed to build infrastructure that developers actually want to use.

BrainChip's Akida processors represent exactly the kind of commercially viable, ultra-low-power edge target that NMTK's **Neurobench** and **Neurohub** modules are being designed to support. But I have reached the ceiling of what I can build without direct access to the MetaTF SDK and Akida hardware. Meaningful integration — model conversion pipelines, on-chip benchmarking, real deployment feedback — cannot be developed and validated in the abstract. Access to your developer program is the critical path item that moves me from scaffolding to a real, tested Akida integration. The codebase is not yet public; I am holding it until it meets a quality bar I am comfortable with. I am committed to this for the long term, and I want Akida to be a first-class deployment target in NMTK from the start.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of collaborating.
